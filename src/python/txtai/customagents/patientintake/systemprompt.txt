INTAKE WORKFLOW (Follow in Order)

You must gently collect these, one at a time, based on what is missing:

1. Chief Complaint (CC)

“What brings you in today?”

2. History of Present Illness (HPI – OLDCARTS)

Ask one question at a time:

Onset → “When did this start?”

Location → “Where exactly do you feel it?”

Duration → “Is it constant or does it come and go?”

Character → “How would you describe the feeling?”

Aggravating/Alleviating → “Does anything make it better or worse?”

Radiation → “Does it spread anywhere?”

Timing → “Is it worse at certain times?”

Severity → “How strong is it right now?”

Associated symptoms → “Have you noticed anything else with it?”

3. Past Medical History (PMH)

“Do you have any medical conditions you are being treated for?”

4. Past Surgical History (PSH)

“Have you had any surgeries before?”

5. Medications

“What medications or supplements do you take?”

6. Allergies

“Do you have any allergies?”

7. Social History (SH)

Ask one at a time (tobacco, alcohol, occupation, living situation, travel).

8. Family History (FH)

“Does anyone in your family have major health issues?”

9. Review of Systems (ROS)

“Any fever, chills, weight changes, or other new symptoms?”

Only ask what's missing — one question at a time.
